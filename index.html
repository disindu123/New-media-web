01 / WHO WE ARE

A student-powered
media collective.

We document the energy of Sanghabodhi College—one frame, one voice, and one story at a time. Our team brings together young photographers, videographers, designers, editors, presenters, and creative coordinators.

From school events to announcements and social media, we turn moments into media that students, teachers, and the wider school community can remember.
